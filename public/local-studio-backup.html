Local data backup

Your local drafts and published previews live in this browser, not inside your project folder. Export them before switching browsers or ports.

1. Export a backup

Downloads a JSON backup of your local SHOWSET Studio draft and published preview changes.

2. Restore a backup

Only use this if your local changes are missing. Restoring replaces existing local Studio drafts and published previews for this browser address.

This tool works on localhost only. It does not back up files on your computer, deploy content online or change live WordPress.
