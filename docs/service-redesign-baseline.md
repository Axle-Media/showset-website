# Service redesign baseline

Starting commit: `8989ea1d3acd51413b60a98f30c7be65beb6556f`, feature branch `feat/cinematic-service-pages`.

Actual Chromium captures cover the four existing service pages at 1440/390 and the live SHOWSET homepage and Atmosphere case study. The Lando Norris reference is used for its progression between immersive photography and editorial pauses, with SHOWSET's existing fonts, navy/teal palette and restraint retained.

| Priority | Finding | Planned correction |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | All four routes use the same category composition and motion. Their disciplines lack distinct narratives. | Four art-directed openings and four different signature interactions. |
| P1 | Capabilities are legitimate and extensive, but compete with repeated conventional section layouts. | Preserve every capability and ID; provide a searchable, keyboard-native expertise index outside animation. |
| P1 | Service binding is positional and dependent on the legacy category markup. | One SSR/Studio service renderer with stable section IDs and independently disposed service motion. |
| P2 | Imagery behaves as generic hero/banner decoration. | Use locally hosted planning, identity and production images as purposeful sequences; use editorial graphic compositions for digital rather than fake devices/analytics. |
| P2 | Mobile inherits the same long desktop sequence. | Dedicated single-column reading compositions, no pinning on narrow/short screens, all content visible without JS or motion. |

Preserve global navigation, header/footer, 14px controls, 17px body, approved headings, instant route scroll-to-top, SVG transition, homepage and case studies. Existing source sections, IDs and capability copy remain available to Studio. No major dependencies, automatic image galleries or production deployment are required.

Distinct signatures: Events transforms a plan into atmosphere; Creative unfolds a graphic proof into applications; Digital connects selectable editorial channel modules; Production follows a scroll-driven photographic contact sequence. The actual renderer and browser captures will be refined section by section before delivery.
